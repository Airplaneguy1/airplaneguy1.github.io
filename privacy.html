Momentum Privacy Statement

Last updated: November 13, 2026. Momentum Core 1.0 for Mac and Windows is expected in December 2026.

This website

This site is a product information page. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sell or share personal information.

Google API Services

Momentum’s project backup tooling (the “momentum-rclone” application) connects to the Google Drive account of the person who sets it up, and only that account. It requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive to create, upload, list and verify encrypted backup files in a folder named MomentumBackup in that same account. Files are encrypted on the owner’s computer before upload.

  • The application accesses only the Google account that the owner signs in with.
  • It does not read, copy or transmit your Google data to the Momentum developers or to any third party. Data goes between the owner’s computer and the owner’s own Google Drive.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, and no person reads it.
  • The application’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • To stop access, remove “momentum-rclone” at myaccount.google.com/permissions; you may delete the MomentumBackup folder at any time.

Data retention and deletion

The Momentum developers store none of your Google data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. The encrypted backup files live in your own Google Drive until you delete them. The application keeps a sign-in token on the computer where it was set up; deleting the application’s configuration, or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, ends its access at once.
